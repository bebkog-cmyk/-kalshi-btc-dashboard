What the model is doing

It estimates one-minute BTC volatility from recent Coinbase candles, shrinks recent momentum toward zero, adjusts the horizon for Kalshi’s final-minute averaging, and converts the result into a probability. The journal is how we test calibration. This prototype is not evidence of a profitable edge.

View-only: no orders are placed and no Kalshi credentials are stored. Public-relay data can be delayed or unavailable and is for paper testing only. Verify the market and rules directly in Kalshi. · Build 0.5
